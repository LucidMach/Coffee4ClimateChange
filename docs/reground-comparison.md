# Reground comparison and Nile's adoption hypothesis

Reviewed 4 October 2026. No quote, private portal inspection or partnership discussion occurred.

Reground is an established alternative and a potential collection partner. Its [grounds service](https://reground.com.au/collection/ground-coffee/) includes bins, pickups, destination tracking, an online impact portal and updates. It also offers [chaff collection](https://reground.com.au/collection/chaff/), [education/consultancy](https://reground.com.au/consultancy/), [Circular Coffee ESG support and its own certification](https://reground.com.au/collection/circular-coffee/), and [software/systems for remote partners](https://reground.com.au/remote/).

Nile demonstrates eligibility before ranking, transparent batch economics, a shared collection brief, separate receipt/use records and CSV purchase planning. Both grounds and **usable surplus beans to a sample cafe** work locally. Reviewed Reground service pages do not explicitly advertise a usable surplus roasted-bean marketplace; this does not prove none exists.

The commercial hypothesis is easier handling of irregular batches and multi-recipient coordination. Test it against actual existing arrangements. Nile has no fleet, real buyers or demonstrated price/climate advantage. Do not claim Reground lacks technology or reporting, only serves agriculture, or is limited to Melbourne.

A collector such as Reground could fulfil the physical step: **potential partner, not contacted or agreed**. Existing successful diversion must not be attributed to Nile.
