# Nile: two-minute recording script

**Required submission asset:** a public or appropriately shareable video URL. No video has been recorded or uploaded by this preparation. Target 1:55 to leave five seconds for a final title card; rehearse and trim against an actual timer.

Use the working local app and fictional test fixtures. Label the demo data and scenario figures on screen. A short dramatized future coffee-shortage shot is optional; label it as fiction and keep it under five seconds so the working product gets most of the video. Do not use “coffee will be dead” or a methane-recapture claim.

## Recording notes

- Pre-position one test batch through the transfer stages, or use edited screen recordings of each stage. Do not attempt the entire uncut handover in two minutes. Preserve your existing database; use an isolated test workspace if recording fresh flows.
- Keep “Sample data” visible on match and value shots. Name the supplier and recipient views as demo role switching, not authenticated real businesses.
- Live AI remains unconfigured. Showing “Rules explanation - no model call” is accurate; do not replace it with an AI-success overlay.
- Skip the national methane figure in the short video unless there is room to state its proxy assumptions and lifetime boundary. Actual impact is not known.
- RMIT source for the opening figure: https://www.rmit.edu.au/news/all-news/2023/aug/coffee-concrete.
