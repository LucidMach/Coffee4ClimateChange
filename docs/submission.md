## Evidence and disclosure

The team reports qualitative interviews with four cafes; some already have agricultural collection for their grounds. These are not verified weights, demand, willingness to pay or pilot results. Existing reuse must not be counted as additional diversion.

The proposed pilot is four weeks with 3-5 nearby cafes and 1-2 recipients, subject to agreement: week 1 establishes starting routes and acceptance requirements; weeks 2-4 test repeat collections. Pickup completion, staff effort, costs, repeat participation and separate offered/accepted/reported-used weights would test adoption and impact. Targets in the description and deck are proposed evaluation gates, not achieved results or commitments.

Businesses, quotes and capacities in the build are fictional fixtures. Accepted quantities, recipient-reported use and national waste/methane illustrations must retain their distinct labels. No real pilot or climate result is claimed. Farmer adaptation, weather and satellite extensions are unbuilt; current Awareness features explain preparation, reuse and climate evidence.

The public demo uses private Vercel Blob workspaces and a hosted Python quality model. Live OpenAI calls are disabled publicly; match explanations use labelled rules. Supabase remains a prepared SQL foundation; hosted Supabase and real account authentication are not connected. No Figma or Canva use was reported by the team.
