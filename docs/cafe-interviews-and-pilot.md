# Early interviews and proposed adoption test

## What the team reported

The team reported **four cafe interviews** on 4 October 2026. Recurring problems were spent grounds placed in bins, occasional excess usable beans and no consistent system covering those materials. Some cafes already have grounds collected for agriculture.

Cafe names, dates, transcripts, weights, bin destinations and commitments were not supplied. This is qualitative problem feedback, not a representative survey, verified landfill outcome or commercial traction.

## Three connected priorities

- **Zero Waste & Methane Reduction:** make additional diversion and prevention practical, compared with the actual existing disposal route.
- **Green Industrialisation:** connect suitable coffee materials with accepting growers/processors that use them as inputs. Real substitution of virgin material needs recipient evidence; it is not automatically established by a transfer.
- **Awareness:** explain preparation, reuse requirements and climate estimates. Test understanding and behaviour. Climate-resilient farm planning, satellite scans and weather advice remain future extensions.

## Proposed four-week pilot

Invite **3–5 nearby cafes and 1–2 accepting recipient organisations**; no participants or partnerships are confirmed.

1. **Week 1:** check disposal destinations, existing services, weights and costs. Confirm recipient capacity, acceptance rules, storage, containers, access, times and responsibility.
2. **Weeks 2–4:** start with one agreed grounds route; introduce usable beans only with recipient-approved requirements. Record offered, accepted and reported-used kg separately, plus time, costs and failures.
3. **Review:** compare additional diversion with baseline and ask whether both sides want another cycle.

Suggested decision gates: at least 80% of agreed pickups complete; median repeat listing takes at most two minutes; at least 60% of cafes choose another cycle; median net benefit is nonnegative after documented incremental costs. These are proposed targets, not results or promises.

For Awareness, check users' understanding of correct preparation and the difference between accepted mass, reported use and estimates before and after the pilot. For Green Industrialisation, ask recipients to record actual use and any displaced input. Use reports remain self-reports unless independently checked.

Credit additional landfill diversion only where the baseline is verified. Existing agricultural/other collection is excluded. Methane remains an estimate using validated material, destination and transport assumptions, not a directly measured pilot outcome.
