# Presentation impact scenario

The deck's **100-cafe scenario is hypothetical**. Four qualitative interviews did not establish weights, actual disposal destinations or willingness to participate.

| Assumption                                 |           Value |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------: |
| Cafes                                      |             100 |
| Eligible otherwise-landfilled grounds      | 20 kg/cafe/week |
| Eligible otherwise-landfilled usable beans |  1 kg/cafe/week |
| Period                                     |        52 weeks |
| Additional successful diversion            |             80% |

Exclude materials already collected for agriculture or another reuse pathway before applying the inputs. The assumed successful diversion produces **83.2 tonnes of grounds/year**, **4.16 tonnes of beans/year** and **87.36 tonnes combined/year**.

The grounds-only illustration assumes landfill-to-compost diversion. [DCCEEW's NGA Factors 2026](https://www.dcceew.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/national-greenhouse-accounts-factors-2026.pdf), Tables 15, 44 and 38, provide the generic food-waste landfill proxy (2.1 t CO2e/t), compost methane component (0.021 t CO2e/t) and CH4 GWP100 (28):

`83.2 × (2.1 × (1 − effective landfill methane capture) − 0.021) / 28`

| Assumed effective capture | Lifetime methane potentially avoided |
| ------------------------- | -----------------------------------: |
| 0%                        |             6.1776 tonnes CH4 (~6.2) |
| 50%                       |             3.0576 tonnes CH4 (~3.1) |
| 70%                       |             1.8096 tonnes CH4 (~1.8) |

These are alternative assumptions, not a confidence interval. This represents **lifetime potential from one year's diverted grounds**, not an annual methane saving or recaptured methane. Coffee-specific composition and wet-weight basis are unvalidated. Transport, processing energy and substitution are excluded; this is not a net lifecycle result. Beans receive **no methane credit** and later brewing grounds must not be counted twice.

The app's existing national-scale opening scenario is separate and unchanged. Its inputs must not be combined with this cohort as though they were related.
