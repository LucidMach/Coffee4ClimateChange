# A five-minute Nile demo

**Opening:** “A café knows what it has left over. Finding someone who can actually use it—and coordinating the handover—is the hard part. Nile compares compatibility, collection and real net value, then records the outcome.”

Before the workflow, show the green opening: **Less coffee waste. Less methane.** Explain the connection to COP31's zero-waste and methane-reduction priority: Nile finds recipients for coffee materials, coordinates transfers and records reported reuse. Coffee bean → latte → spent grounds loops while you stay at the top.

In **Australia**, move the slider from 1% to 1.5%. At 1%, the scenario shows **750 tonnes of grounds potentially kept out of landfill**, **about 55.7 tonnes of methane potentially avoided over that waste's lifetime**, and an independent **outreach target of about 282 café businesses**. At 1.5%, those become 1,125 tonnes, about 83.5 tonnes methane and about 422 businesses. These are projections, not measured impact or contacts. Reaching a share of cafés does not guarantee an equal share of grounds diverted. The methane estimate uses generic food-waste proxies, assumes no landfill gas capture and excludes transport. It is not methane recaptured or emissions saved in a single year.

Open **Assumptions & sources** to inspect sources, inputs and the COP31 connection. Switch to **Our records** for the local evidence: waste reuse and beans kept in use reflect recipient reports, while actual CO₂e benefit remains unknown. Scroll slowly to carry the grounds toward the circles; scroll back to reverse the fall and resume the loop. Click **Overview** to reveal the working panel.

1. **Supplier overview.** Say that the businesses, requirements and quotes are sample data. Open this morning's 30 kg grounds batch.
2. **Compare options.** Loop Mushroom Co. meets its sample requirements. The nearby grower cannot accept a 30 kg batch because its sample minimum is 50 kg. Compost has a collection fee. The material lab is an unconfirmed prospect and cannot be booked. Explain why highest quoted price or nearest distance alone is insufficient.
3. **Show the economics.** Sample revenue is AUD12, handling is AUD2, and avoided disposal cost is zero because the fixed bill does not change: AUD10 net benefit under those terms. Actual payment is not processed in the demo.
4. **Prepare and agree collection.** Propose the handover. Add pickup contact, access instructions and container count to the pre-filled shared brief; review the recipient’s preparation rules and mark the café ready. Use “Review as recipient” to confirm handling, transport and a pickup time within the window. Download the brief. A revision clears recipient approval until reviewed again. Demo role switching stands in for separate authenticated accounts.
5. **Record the actual receipt.** Enter 27 kg received. As supplier, review and confirm it. The remaining 3 kg becomes available again. A disputed receipt would stay excluded from completed totals.
6. **Report use separately.** As recipient, report 25 kg used in mushroom substrate. Explain that this is a recipient report, not independent certification.
7. **Show beans.** Open 6 kg surplus roasted beans and show Second Shot Café's packaging and labelled-date checks. The same engine handles a different material and recipient requirement set.
8. **Show network impact.** Grounds example: 27 kg transferred, 25 kg reported used. The two totals stay distinct. Carbon benefit remains unknown until actual disposal, processing and transport inputs are validated.

9. **Show a reason to return.** In Overview, open **Plan next bean order** and choose **Try fictional sample**. With a 14-day plan, 5 kg usable stock, 3 kg confirmed incoming, a two-day buffer, 2 kg packs and AUD30/kg, Nile suggests 50 kg costing AUD1,500. Against a 60 kg original plan and AUD1,800 budget, the difference is AUD300. Increase the business goal to show that a bigger order can cost more. This is an illustrative planning comparison, not money already saved. A real CSV of completed daily totals replaces the sample; no customer data or OpenAI upload is needed.
10. **Show scoped recognition.** After confirmed transfers, download the Nile participation record from Overview. It names the demo evidence and keeps accepted weight separate from recipient-reported use. Say “COP31-aligned waste action”, not “COP31-certified café”. Independent certification remains a separate process.

**Theme connection:** Zero Waste & Methane Reduction is the core intervention; Green Industrialization extends the same workflow to secondary materials. Awareness comes from explaining requirements, value and evidence. Climate impact is supported by a measured change of route and a validated comparison, not a generic claim per kilogram.

**Close:** “Tell Nile what you have. It finds compatible options, shows what you gain after costs and makes the handover easier. Our next step is a small pilot with real recipients, quotes and disposal baselines.”

The in-app “Walk through the demo” button contains the same sequence. To show a clean demonstration again, use a **new** `NILE_DB_PATH` and restart the local server; retain the previous database rather than deleting recorded work.
